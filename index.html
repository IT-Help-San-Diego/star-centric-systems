The keystone: Star-Centric Transport

Star-Centric Transport is the keystone this roof is named for. On the public record it carries the verify-before-you-advance discipline of the Verification Principle into data pipelines: a chunk counts as progress only while a verifier can still recover its center. The roof's own aim is wider, and this page states it rather than cites it. The aim is to carry a verified signal intact through every hop that can distort it: a carrier, an observer, an institution, a machine, a civilization, and time.

The unit it carries is the packet.

P = ⟨D, C, L⟩

D is the payload. C is the carrier-color tags that rode along with it. L is a signed, append-only log of every hop.

The rule is checked at every checkpoint. What arrives is the center deformed by its context. The verifier estimates the center, conditioned on the carrier tags C, and lets the chunk advance only while that estimate stays inside the tolerance band.

xi = f(ci, ki)  ·  advance iff ‖ĉi,C − ci‖ ≤ τ

Outside the band the chunk is slowed, quarantined, or flagged. It is not counted as progress.

Every hop leaves a record a stranger can replay, so where a presupposition entered is found by replay, not by forensic guess.

Proposal
On the public record Star-Centric Transport is a design proposal with a worked formalism. It is not a deployed protocol with benchmarks.

The Verification Principle

Proven (theorem)

A belief pinned at certainty or impossibility cannot learn. If the prior is exactly 0 or exactly 1, Bayes' rule returns it unchanged.

P(E) > 0, P(H) ∈ {0, 1} ⇒ P(H | E) = P(H)

The discipline is to set priors from evidence, keep them strictly between 0 and 1, and update in odds form so evidence can always move them.

Carrier Color

Model / framework

A message arrives as a signal and a carrier. The model's claim is that the carrier is often judged first: Received = Signal + Carrier Color.

The test is to strip the carrier and see whether the evaluation of the signal changes. The aim is not to delete the carrier but to make it legible.

The transport machinery: the record

The record is the transport. On this roof a claim counts as carried only when it leaves a record a stranger can replay; Star-Centric Transport, the protocol, remains a proposal. Four pieces of machinery make the record: one is specified, three are running.

Append-only ledger
Specified in the packet as the lineage log L: a signed, append-only record of each hop, written once and never rewritten. A later entry can answer an earlier one, but it cannot erase it. No deployed ledger is on the public record yet.
SHA-3 seals
A seal is the hash of a result, taken the moment the result exists. It is tamper-evidence: it shows the result you hold is the one that was sealed, and it does not prove the measurement occurred. Calibration Scope seals every run with SHA-3 and pins every stimulus by hash. Resolution Scope seals each verdict under the scheme resolution-scope-sha3-512-v5 and prints the seal and the scheme at the head of each report.
Zenodo DOIs
Three works carry Zenodo DOIs. Each version has its own DOI, and a concept DOI resolves to the latest version; this page links the concept DOIs. DNS Tool: 10.5281/zenodo.19468134 ↗ (concept DOI). Owl Semaphore: 10.5281/zenodo.19473697 ↗ (concept DOI). Tetrasemaphore: 10.5281/zenodo.23575814 ↗ (concept DOI).
Internet Archive
DNS Tool submits the URL of each public report to the Internet Archive, which keeps a record outside the family's control.
